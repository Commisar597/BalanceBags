Hey there!

We’ve all had that urge to play Minecraft with friends over
the summer, right?

I know you have—especially since you’re reading this right now! ;)

Well, I felt the same way. My friend loves playing with backpack mods,
but I’m not really a fan. The issue isn't the backpacks themselves,
but rather that—in my opinion—they’re usually way too overpowered.

Luckily for me, I had just finished my first year of studies when I started
working on this mod, so I already had a decent grasp of coding (^-^).

So, I went ahead and created the models, designed the textures... and within a week,
I had created some bags that I’m actually happy with.

Now, getting to the point: my bags add a touch of realism compared to most alternatives.
They don’t have an excessive storage capacity, and if you overload them, they’ll actually
slow the player down. But if you need to carry a whole lot of stuff, you can equip
yourself with bags from head to toe—well, maybe "head" is an exaggeration...

From  your shoulders to your toes!

If you decide to play this, I hope you enjoy it. So, have fun—and if you find any bugs,
let me know. I'll do my best to fix them)
